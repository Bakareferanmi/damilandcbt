Academic session

Every session has its own students, questions and results, and student numbers start again at 001. Only students of the current session can log in. Old sessions stay saved and can be viewed (read-only) from the Session menu in the sidebar.

Start a new session

The current session’s students will no longer be able to log in, so do this only when the new school year begins. Nothing is deleted.

Exam length

How long students have for the whole exam (all four subjects). A change applies to students who start after you save. Anyone already sitting keeps their time.

Administrators

People who can sign in to this admin area.
